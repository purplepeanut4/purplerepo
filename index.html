AppTunes 2.0.0

Play a different song in each app you choose. Import your own MP3s and pick which apps they play in, all from Settings.

iOS 12+TweakNeeds PreferenceLoader
Download .deb

How to use AppTunes

  1. Install AppTunes, then respring.
  2. Open Settings → AppTunes.
  3. Tap Import a Song... and pick your MP3s.
  4. Scroll to Apps, tap an app, and choose its song.
