  2. TapLock1.0.0

    Double-tap to lock.

    Double-tap an empty spot on the Home Screen or Dock to lock your device, so you never have to reach for the power button.

    details
    • Works on the Home Screen and the Dock
    • Ignores taps on icons, widgets and folders
    • Each area can be switched off separately

    Settings > TapLock

    download .deb

  5. BatteryLine1.0.0

    A battery meter you can see in any app.

    A thin line along the top or bottom of the screen that shows your battery level on top of every app. Turns green while charging, orange under 50% and red under 20%.

    details
    • Top or bottom edge
    • Adjustable thickness and opacity
    • Automatic or fixed colour
    • Optional pulse while charging
    • Can show only while charging

    Settings > BatteryLine

Themes5

Icon themes for Snowboard or Anemone. Covers the stock Apple apps plus Sileo, Zebra, Cydia and Filza. Apps without an icon here keep their normal one.

Using the repo

  1. Open this page on your iPhone and tap Add to Sileo, or paste the address into Sources.
  2. Pull down in Sileo to refresh, then install what you like.
  3. Respring. Tweaks show up in Settings; themes are applied from Snowboard or Anemone.
