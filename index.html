Works with5

Any jailbreak should work if it gives you iOS 12 on an arm64 device, a rootful filesystem, a tweak injector (Substrate, Substitute or libhooker) and PreferenceLoader.

  • checkra1niOS 12.0 to 14.8.1 A7 to A11 chips: iPhone 5s up to iPhone X, plus iPads and iPods with the same chips

    Semi-tethered: run it from a Mac or Linux PC again after every reboot. Rootful on iOS 12, so everything installs the normal way. The main target for this repo.

  • Odysseyra1niOS 12.0 to 14.8.1 Same devices as checkra1n

    Runs on top of checkra1n and also needs a computer after every reboot. It uses libhooker where older jailbreaks use Substrate; libhooker stands in for it.

  • AmethystiOS 12.0 to 13.7 arm64 devices (listed for all devices on those versions)

    Released June 2026. Semi-untethered: sideload the app and tap to re-jailbreak after a reboot. It loads tweaks, and a community write-up reports it working on an iPhone 6 running iOS 12.5.8.

  • unc0veriOS 12.0 to 12.4.8 (skips 12.3 to 12.3.2) A7 to A11 chips, partial support on A12

    Does not cover iOS 12.5.x. If you are on 12.5.x, use one of the others.

  • ChimeraiOS 12.0 to 12.5.7 A7 to A12 chips

    Comes with Substitute and Sileo. iOS 12.5.x needs the community chimera_patch.

Not compatibleOdyssey (iOS 13), Taurine (iOS 14), Dopamine, palera1n and XinaA15 (iOS 15 and up), and any jailbreak for iOS 13 or later. Amethyst and Odysseyra1n can reach those versions, but these packages are only built for iOS 12 and rootful systems.

Version ranges checked against public sources in October 2026. They can change, so confirm on each project's own page. These packages were not tested on every one.

Using the repo

  1. Open this page on your iPhone and tap Add to Sileo, or paste the address into Sources.
  2. Pull down in Sileo to refresh, then install what you like.
  3. Respring. Tweaks show up in Settings; themes are applied from Snowboard or Anemone.
